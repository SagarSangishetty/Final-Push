===========================================
Auto Scaling Group (ASG)
===========================================

What is ASG?

Auto Scaling Group automatically launches, terminates, and replaces EC2 instances.

It ensures the desired number of healthy instances are always available.

--------------------------------------------------

Responsibilities

ALB

↓

Distributes traffic.

----------------------------

ASG

↓

Manages EC2 instances.

--------------------------------------------------

Three Important Values

Minimum Capacity

- Minimum number of EC2 instances.

Desired Capacity

- Number of EC2 instances ASG tries to maintain.

Maximum Capacity

- Maximum number of EC2 instances ASG can launch.

--------------------------------------------------

↓

↓

↓

↓

Health Check

↓

--------------------------------------------------

Scaling In

CloudWatch

↓

CPU < 20%

↓

Scaling Policy

↓

Terminate EC2

↓

↓

↓

Terminate Instance

--------------------------------------------------

If EC2 Crashes

ALB

↓

Marks target unhealthy

↓

Stops sending traffic

↓

--------------------------------------------------

Interview Answer

Auto Scaling Group automatically launches, terminates, and replaces EC2 instances based on scaling policies or health checks. It works together with CloudWatch, Target Groups, and Application Load Balancers to maintain application availability and scale according to demand.
