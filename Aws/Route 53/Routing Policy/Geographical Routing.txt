========================================================

Route 53 - Geolocation Routing
===========================================

What is Geolocation Routing?
----------------------------

Geolocation Routing directs users based on their geographic location.

--------------------------------------------------

Example

India

↓

Indian Website

----------------------------

USA

↓

US Website

----------------------------

Europe

↓

EU Website

--------------------------------------------------

Interview Answer

Geolocation Routing directs traffic based on the user's geographic location, such as country or continent. It is useful when different regions require different content or applications.
