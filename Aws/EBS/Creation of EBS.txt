EBS -- 

How it is created in companies.

Usually volumes are created mount based. 

Example - 

EC2 Instance
│
├── Root EBS Volume (100 GB)
│      ├── /
│      ├── /etc
│      ├── /usr
│      └── /var
│
├── EBS Volume 2 (500 GB)
│      └── /data
│
├── EBS Volume 3 (1 TB)
│      └── /backup
│
└── EBS Volume 4 (Database Logs)
       └── /db-logs

Isolation : If logs are full OS mount is not impacted.

Different Performance Requirements : Different workloads require different EBS types.
